# 按年龄与年级选择

下表是编辑建议，不是认证课程映射或入学年龄规则。各州截止日期及孩子准备程度不同。Foundation 在不同地区也称 Prep、Reception、Kindergarten、Transition 或 Pre-primary。英国学段、美国年级和 CEFR 等级不直接等于澳洲年级。EAL 学习者可降低语言难度，同时保留适龄主题。

| 澳洲阶段 | 大致年龄 | 可尝试的阅读任务 | 资源 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Preschool | 3–5 | 看图说话，复述一件事 | [Literacy Hub](https://www.literacyhub.edu.au/for-families/) · [Oxford Owl for Home](https://www.oxfordowl.co.uk/for-home/library-page) · [Queensland libraries: LOTE4Kids](https://www.slq.qld.gov.au/collections/public-library-collections/collections-all-queensland-public-libraries) |
| Foundation | 5–6 | 听辨语音，在成人帮助下拼合已教字母音 | [Literacy Hub](https://www.literacyhub.edu.au/for-families/) · [Teach Your Monster to Read](https://www.teachyourmonster.org/teach-your-monster-to-read-overview/) · [Oxford Owl for Home](https://www.oxfordowl.co.uk/for-home/library-page) · [UFLI Foundations Toolbox](https://ufli.education.ufl.edu/foundations/toolbox/) |
| Year 1 | 6–7 | 读难度匹配的短文，按顺序复述 | [Literacy Hub](https://www.literacyhub.edu.au/for-families/) · [Teach Your Monster to Read](https://www.teachyourmonster.org/teach-your-monster-to-read-overview/) · [UFLI Foundations Toolbox](https://ufli.education.ufl.edu/foundations/toolbox/) |
| Year 2 | 7–8 | 重复朗读改善语句停顿，结合上下文解释词义 | [Literacy Hub](https://www.literacyhub.edu.au/for-families/) · [Oxford Owl for Home](https://www.oxfordowl.co.uk/for-home/library-page) · [FCRR Student Center Activities](https://fcrr.org/student-center-activities) |
| Year 3 | 8–9 | 找出主旨及一条支持细节 | [Oxford Owl for Home](https://www.oxfordowl.co.uk/for-home/library-page) · [LearnEnglish Kids](https://learnenglishkids.britishcouncil.org/read-write/reading-practice) · [FCRR Student Center Activities](https://fcrr.org/student-center-activities) |
| Year 4 | 9–10 | 引用线索推断人物感受 | [Storyline Online](https://storylineonline.net/) · [LearnEnglish Kids](https://learnenglishkids.britishcouncil.org/read-write/reading-practice) · [FCRR Student Center Activities](https://fcrr.org/student-center-activities) |
| Year 5 | 10–11 | 比较两个人物或两份事实材料 | [LearnEnglish Kids](https://learnenglishkids.britishcouncil.org/read-write/reading-practice) · [StoryWeaver — Pratham Books](https://storyweaver.org.in/en/) · [ABC BTN](https://www.abc.net.au/btn/classroom) |
| Year 6 | 11–12 | 区分观点陈述与支持证据 | [LearnEnglish Kids](https://learnenglishkids.britishcouncil.org/read-write/reading-practice) · [ABC BTN](https://www.abc.net.au/btn/classroom) · [ABC Education: Learn English](https://www.abc.net.au/education/learn-english) |
| Year 7 | 12–13 | 用自己的话概括一段内容 | [LearnEnglish Kids](https://learnenglishkids.britishcouncil.org/read-write/reading-practice) · [ABC BTN](https://www.abc.net.au/btn/classroom) · [ABC Education: Learn English](https://www.abc.net.au/education/learn-english) |
| Year 8 | 13–14 | 说明视角并选出支持引文 | [LearnEnglish Teens](https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/skills/reading) · [ABC BTN](https://www.abc.net.au/btn/classroom) · [ABC Education: Learn English](https://www.abc.net.au/education/learn-english) |
| Year 9 | 14–15 | 比较两种来源如何讲述同一事件 | [LearnEnglish Teens](https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/skills/reading) · [ABC BTN](https://www.abc.net.au/btn/classroom) · [ABC Education: Learn English](https://www.abc.net.au/education/learn-english) |
| Year 10 | 15–16 | 评价论点并发现缺失的视角 | [LearnEnglish Teens](https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/skills/reading) · [ABC BTN](https://www.abc.net.au/btn/classroom) · [ABC Education: Learn English](https://www.abc.net.au/education/learn-english) |
| Year 11 | 16–17 | 用证据提出解读，并考虑另一种解释 | [LearnEnglish Teens](https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/skills/reading) · [ABC Education: Learn English](https://www.abc.net.au/education/learn-english) |
| Year 12 | 17–18 | 按实际课程评分标准修订持续论证 | [LearnEnglish Teens](https://learnenglishteens.britishcouncil.org/skills/reading) · [ABC Education: Learn English](https://www.abc.net.au/education/learn-english) |
